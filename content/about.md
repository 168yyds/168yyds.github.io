Hi, I’m **Guangchen Wu**, also known as **Yichi**, originally from Anhui, China. I received my B.Eng. degree in **Energy Storage Science and Engineering** from the **School of Future Technology, Xi’an Jiaotong University**, where I studied from September 2021 to June 2025. Since September 2025, I have been pursuing my graduate studies in **Power Electronics** at the **School of Electrical Engineering, Xi’an Jiaotong University**.

## Research interests

My research focuses on the **application of artificial intelligence in power electronics**. My current research interests include:

- **Machine learning for impedance modeling of grid-connected inverters**
- **48 V data-center DC power systems: stability analysis and stabilizing control of DAB converters**
- **AI for Power Electronics**

I am particularly interested in combining data-driven and AI-based methods with conventional modeling and control techniques to address practical challenges in modern power electronic systems.
