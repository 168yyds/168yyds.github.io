Welcome! I’m **Guangchen Wu (Yichi)**, a graduate student in Power Electronics at Xi’an Jiaotong University.

This blog is a place to collect learning notes and explore ideas at the intersection of **power electronics** and **artificial intelligence**.

## Topics I’m exploring

- Machine learning for impedance modeling of grid-connected inverters
- 48 V data-center DC power systems: stability analysis and stabilizing control of DAB converters
- AI for Power Electronics

My interests connect conventional engineering methods with tools that learn from data. I hope these notes will become a useful record of that learning process.
